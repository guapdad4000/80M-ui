## Rules

1. Every expense and every sale gets a row. No exceptions, no rounding up.
2. Platform fees are logged as costs, not hidden in "net."
3. Refunds are logged as negative revenue.
4. Free tools are noted in the weekly update so the "$0" is honest.
5. Off-budget costs (like the AI subscription) are listed separately and disclosed in every weekly update.
6. Human time is tracked in the weekly update (hours), because time isn't free either.
