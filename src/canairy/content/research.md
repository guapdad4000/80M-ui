## Patterns (our reading, not the sources' claims)

1. **Real-money, real-customer runs mostly lost money or made little** (1, 2, 9, 11). The headline wins are tokens, fundraising, or simulations (3, 6, 7).
2. **The common failure is being too agreeable**: discounts, giveaways and refunds to whoever asks (1, 2, 4).
3. **Guardrails worked where they existed**: spending caps (9), CEO approval rules (2).
4. **Attention is what came first.** HustleGPT and Felix both started with a person who already had an audience on X.
5. **Clerical web tasks are a real bottleneck**: forms, file sharing, logins (7, 10).
