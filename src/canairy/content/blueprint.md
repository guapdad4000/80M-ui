# Blueprint ideas

The free, audience-and-content half of the original business blueprint. Everything here is free.

## 3. Audience

### 3.1 Profile (primary)
**"The skeptical tinkerer."** Someone who uses AI assistants weekly, has a day job, has started or wanted to start a side project, and is tired of "AI made me $10k/month" content. Developer, PM, designer, marketer, or technical hobbyist. Comfortable with GitHub, or willing to be.

**Secondary:** people curious about *agentic* workflows ("can an AI really run things?") who follow along for the experiment and may buy later.

### 3.2 Problems, goals, pain points

| Problems | Goals | Pain points |
|---|---|---|
| Don't know which idea is worth testing | Launch *something* real with little money | Guru content with fake or unverifiable numbers |
| Tool sprawl; subscriptions add up | A repeatable weekly system that fits in a few hours | Fear of an AI doing something embarrassing or costly in their name |
| AI output is generic without a process | Learn agentic workflows by doing | Fees, taxes and payouts they didn't plan for |
| Little time | Some honest proof of what works | Starting, stalling, and abandoning |

### 3.3 Where they hang out (and how we show up)
- **X:** build-in-public and indie-hacker circles, AI tooling discussions. *Primary channel.*
- **GitHub:** discovery via topics/stars; the repo is our "storefront window."
- **Reddit:** r/SideProject, r/indiehackers, r/Entrepreneur, AI tool subreddits. **Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules first.** Post the story and lessons; link only where allowed.
- **Indie Hackers** forum, **Hacker News** (a single "Show HN" for the repo, when it's genuinely useful).
- **Newsletters:** other small AI/indie newsletters for swaps or recommendations later.
- **YouTube / TikTok / LinkedIn:** secondary. Repurpose only, from Week 3+.

### 3.4 Best platforms (priority)
1. **X** (daily, short, ledger-driven). 2. **GitHub** (the product sample). 3. **Email** (the only channel we own). 4. **Reddit/IH/HN** (occasional, high-effort posts). 5. **LinkedIn / YouTube Shorts / TikTok** (repurposed clips; faceless screen + canary art).

### 3.5 Messaging angles
1. **The ledger angle:** "Every dollar in and out, including fees and the zeros."
2. **The human-gate angle:** "AI does the work; a human approves anything that spends, signs, or speaks."
3. **The canary angle:** "Early warnings: what breaks, what costs money, before you find out yourself."
4. **The constraint angle:** "$150. 3–5 hours a week. That's it."
5. **The anti-guru angle:** "No income claims. Here's what it actually cost."

### 3.6 Validate demand quickly, before writing more chapters
Run these in the first 14 days. The thresholds are **our own decision rules**, not industry benchmarks.

| Signal | How | Continue if (by Day 14) | Rethink if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email signups | Landing page + repo README + X | ≥25 subscribers | <10 → messaging or channel problem |
| Repo interest | Stars, forks, issues | Any forks/issues from strangers | No traffic → distribution problem, not product |
| Reply/DM sentiment | 5-question poll + DMs | People ask for specific chapters | Replies are "cool idea" only |
| Coalbird overlap | Note any "isn't this Coalbird?" replies | Rare | Frequent → sharpen positioning (§3.7) |


### 3.7 Competitor note: Coalbird (coalbird.co)
What it actually is (checked 20 Oct 2026): an **agent-experience testing service for e-commerce stores**. It sends AI shopping agents through stores and reports where they fail. Plans start at **$79/month** with a free trial ([coalbird.co](https://coalbird.co)). It runs "in public" through an **AI CEO** that publishes board reports on Substack ([about](https://coalbird.co/about), [Coalbird HQ](https://log.coalbird.co/)), and uses the same **canary/mine imagery** ("Send the bird in first").

- **Overlap:** canary metaphor, "AI CEO operating in public," bird mascot.
- **Actions:** (1) Never borrow their phrases ("send the bird in first," "board report," "AI CEO memo"). Our vocabulary is *ledger, human gate, early warning, the dig.* (2) Lead with the $150 ledger, which they don't do. (3) Don't knock them publicly. If asked, "different bird, different mine: they test stores for AI shoppers; we publish what it costs to run a tiny AI-operated business." (4) The Owner may want a quick trademark/name check before spending on a domain (see decisions).

### 4.3 Formats & hooks by platform

**X (daily, 1 post + replies; weekly thread)**
- Formats: single post with a number, ledger screenshot, 5–7 post thread, poll.
- Hooks:
  - "Day 4. Balance: $150.00. Revenue: $0.00. Here's what the AI shipped anyway:"
  - "An AI isn't allowed to do 3 things in this business. Here they are."
  - "The free email plan I almost picked can't send a welcome sequence. Early warning 🐤"

**YouTube (Shorts first; long-form later)**
- Formats: 30–60s screen recording + canary overlay + captions; monthly 8–10 min "ledger review" (Week 5+).
- Hooks: "This AI has $150 and one rule." · "Watch an AI write its own expense report." · "Here's every dollar this business made this month (it's not many)."

**TikTok (repurposed Shorts)**
- Hooks: "POV: your business partner is an AI and it has to ask permission to spend $5." · "Day 7 of letting an AI run a business on $150."

**LinkedIn (weekly, repurposed)**
- Formats: text post with a lesson + one number; carousel PDF of the week's ledger.
- Hooks: "We put a human approval gate on an AI-run business. Here's what it blocked this week." · "Transparency isn't screenshots of revenue. It's fees, refunds, and hours too."

**Newsletter (weekly "The Ledger")**
- Format: (1) the numbers table, (2) what shipped, (3) one early warning, (4) one prompt/routine, (5) one soft link to the playbook.
- Subject-line hooks: "Week 2: $150 → $150 (and why)" · "The fee nobody mentions" · "What the human said no to"

## 8. Rules of the business

1. **Realistic, not hype.** No "passive income," no fake urgency, no "secrets."
2. **Own numbers only.** Show only Canairy's real ledger, including costs, fees, refunds, and Owner hours. Never others' earnings. Never promise buyers results ([FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)).
3. **Disclose.** The AI authorship and the human gate are always disclosed. Any future affiliate link is labeled and logged.
4. **Low cost.** Free tiers first; every spend has a hypothesis and a kill date.
5. **The human gate.** The Owner approves *before*: publishing anything, spending any amount, sending DMs/emails, changing prices, issuing refunds, creating accounts, accepting terms, or anything irreversible.
6. **No invented statistics.** No source, no number. Unverified claims are tagged [VERIFY] and cut before publishing.
7. **Never edit past ledger rows.** Corrections are new rows.
8. **Protect Owner time.** If a week's approvals exceed 5 hours, the AI cuts scope, not corners.

### 8.1 $150 budget allocation (explicit)

| Bucket | Amount | When | Rule |

| **Total** | **$150** | | Spent so far: $0 |

Revenue doesn't top up the budget automatically. The Owner decides whether profits are reinvested, and the ledger shows that decision.
